Guiding Principle

Human authority remains final.

“Help people find what they need without taking authority away from them.”

NAIB can organize, explain, coordinate, research, prepare, and propose. A proposal is not an execution, and final decisions remain with the person using the system.

Public Boundary

A public front, not an authority plane.

NAIB is the canonical public Administrator. Protected intelligence, memory, specialist routing, capability mediation, and consequential authority remain behind governed sovereign boundaries.
