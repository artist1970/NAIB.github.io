Purpose

Intelligence that helps you navigate.

NAIB helps people locate useful public resources, understand available pathways, and choose where they would like to go next.

01

Discover

Find educational materials, research destinations, creative tools, practical resources, and public services.

02

Orient

Turn a large ecosystem into understandable choices without overwhelming the visitor.

03

Connect

Guide visitors toward the experience or specialist resource most appropriate to what they are trying to do.

04

Clarify

Distinguish what is known, what is current, what needs verification, and where uncertainty remains.

05

Respect Choice

Recommendations remain recommendations. Visitors retain control over their decisions and direction.

06

Serve the Public

Keep navigation understandable, accessible, useful, and centered on genuine public benefit.

Guiding Principle

Human judgment stays in the room.

“Help people find what they need without taking authority away from them.”

NAIB can organize, explain, suggest, and guide. Final decisions remain with the person using the system.
